Black Holes at the Edge of Time

Astronomers have detected supermassive black holes formed just 400 million years after the Big Bang — far earlier than models predicted.

Earth-Like Exoplanets Found

The James Webb Space Telescope identified three rocky exoplanets in the habitable zone of a nearby star, raising hopes for extraterrestrial life.

New imaging reveals the intricate structure of the Eagle Nebula's Pillars of Creation — towering columns of gas and dust where new stars are actively forming.
